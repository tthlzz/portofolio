about me

I'm Ahsanul, an IT student who likes turning messy problems into things that actually run. My thesis taught a model to read emotion from human voice; the rest of my time goes into shipping web apps that real people use.

I enjoy both ends of that range. I care about clean, reliable systems: transactions that don't corrupt, data that stays honest. But I also like building odd, curious things just because they're interesting, like linked-data graphs and small game experiments.

Speech Emotion Recognition

Teaches a model to pick up on emotion in speech itself, not just the words, so a system can tell frustration from calm instead of treating every voice the same. The CNN-BiLSTM ensemble reaches 81.71% accuracy on voices it's never heard before.
